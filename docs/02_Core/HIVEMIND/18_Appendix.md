# 1. Purpose

This appendix provides supplementary material supporting the Atlas Hivemind Specification.

The appendix contains reference information, diagrams, examples, mappings, and historical context that assist readers in understanding and implementing the specification.

The appendix is informative and introduces no additional normative requirements.

# 7. Design Principles

The Atlas Hivemind is founded on the following principles.

- Engineering Understanding is the architectural core.
- Evidence precedes conclusions.
- Context gives evidence meaning.
- Reasoning shall be explainable.
- Human engineers retain authority.
- Knowledge evolves continuously.
- Architecture is technology-independent.
- Historical understanding is preserved.
- Engineering disciplines collaborate through shared understanding.

---

# 8. Conformance Checklist

A conformant implementation should verify that it:

- Maintains Engineering Understanding.
- Preserves Architectural Invariants.
- Operates within Architectural Constraints.
- Respects defined Non-Goals.
- Maintains explainability.
- Preserves evidence traceability.
- Preserves engineering context.
- Supports human authority.
- Supports continuous learning.
- Evolves without violating architectural identity.
