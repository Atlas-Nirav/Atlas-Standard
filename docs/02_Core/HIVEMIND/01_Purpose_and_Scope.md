# 1. Purpose

This specification defines the purpose and scope of the Atlas Hivemind.

It establishes why the Hivemind exists, the architectural responsibilities it fulfills, and the boundaries within which it operates.

The purpose of this specification is to provide a shared understanding of the Hivemind before defining its architecture, behavior, responsibilities, and interactions with the rest of the Atlas Engineering Cognition Model.

This document is normative.

Future specifications SHALL conform to the purpose and scope defined herein.

---

# 2. Why the Hivemind Exists

Engineering projects generate knowledge from many independent sources.

These include, but are not limited to:

- Architectural models
- Structural models
- Mechanical systems
- Electrical systems
- Plumbing systems
- BIM models
- CAD drawings
- Engineering calculations
- Construction observations
- Site inspections
- Regulations
- Building codes
- Standards
- Simulations
- Sensor data
- Human expertise
- Historical projects

Each source provides valuable information.

None independently represents the engineering comprehension of an entire project.

Engineering decisions require more than isolated information.

They require understanding.

The Hivemind exists to transform validated engineering evidence into one continuously evolving Engineering Understanding.

---

# 3. Purpose of the Hivemind

The Hivemind serves one primary architectural purpose.

> **Continuously create, maintain, validate, preserve, and evolve the Engineering Understanding of every project.**

Every architectural responsibility of the Hivemind ultimately supports this purpose.

The Hivemind is therefore the Engineering Mind of Atlas.

---

# 4. Scope

The scope of the Hivemind includes the continuous maintenance of Engineering Understanding throughout the lifecycle of an engineering project.

This includes:

- Maintaining project awareness.
- Maintaining engineering context.
- Preserving design intent.
- Integrating validated engineering evidence.
- Coordinating Engineering Capabilities.
- Preserving engineering memory.
- Maintaining engineering relationships.
- Supporting engineering reasoning.
- Preserving explainability.
- Maintaining traceability.
- Supporting continuous learning.
- Preserving engineering consistency.

These responsibilities collectively ensure that Atlas maintains one coherent Engineering Understanding for every project.

---

# 5. Out of Scope

The Hivemind specification intentionally does not define implementation details.

Specifically, this specification does not define:

- Artificial intelligence models.
- Large Language Models (LLMs).
- Machine learning algorithms.
- Graph database implementations.
- Vector databases.
- Workflow orchestration.
- Agent frameworks.
- Runtime environments.
- Deployment architectures.
- Cloud infrastructure.
- Programming languages.
- Communication protocols.
- User interfaces.

These concerns belong to implementation and supporting architecture specifications.

---

# 6. Architectural Boundaries

The Hivemind occupies the cognitive layer of the Atlas Engineering Cognition Model.

It does not replace specialized engineering disciplines.

Instead, it coordinates and integrates them through shared Engineering Understanding.

Engineering Capabilities perform specialized reasoning.

The Hivemind preserves shared cognition.

This separation of responsibilities is fundamental to the Atlas architecture.

---

# 7. Guiding Principle

The Hivemind exists to maintain understanding rather than information.

Data becomes evidence.

Evidence contributes to understanding.

Understanding enables reasoning.

Reasoning supports engineering decisions.

Engineering decisions generate knowledge.

Knowledge strengthens future understanding.

This continuous cycle defines the cognitive purpose of the Hivemind.

---

# 8. Success Criteria

The purpose of the Hivemind is considered fulfilled when:

- Every project maintains one Engineering Understanding.
- Engineering Understanding evolves continuously.
- Engineering decisions remain explainable.
- Engineering reasoning remains traceable.
- Engineering knowledge accumulates across projects.
- Engineering Capabilities collaborate through shared understanding.
- Human engineers remain central to engineering decision making.

---

Subsequent specifications define:

---

# 10. Summary

The Hivemind exists to ensure that Atlas continuously develops and maintains a coherent Engineering Understanding of every project.

It is not responsible for performing engineering work.

It is responsible for maintaining the engineering cognition through which specialized Engineering Capabilities collaborate.

This purpose defines the architectural role of the Hivemind throughout the Atlas Engineering Cognition Model.

---

> **The Hivemind exists to preserve understanding, not merely information.**
