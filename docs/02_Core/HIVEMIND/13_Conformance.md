# HS-013: Conformance

**Specification ID:** HS-013

**Title:** Conformance

**Specification:** Atlas Hivemind Specification (AHS)

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Draft

**Layer:** Governance

**Parent Specification:** HS-009 – Reference Architecture

**Depends On:**
- HS-009 – Reference Architecture
- HS-010 – Architectural Invariants
- HS-011 – Architectural Constraints
- HS-012 – Non-Goals

---

# 1. Purpose

This specification defines the conformance requirements for implementations of the Atlas Hivemind.

Conformance establishes the minimum architectural requirements that an implementation SHALL satisfy to claim compliance with the Atlas Hivemind Specification.

Conformance is evaluated against architectural behavior rather than implementation technology.

---

# 2. Definition

A conformant implementation preserves the architectural intent, governance, and cognitive behavior defined by this specification.

Conformance is determined by observable architectural properties rather than programming language, infrastructure, deployment model, or artificial intelligence technology.

---

# 3. Conformance Principles

Every conformant implementation SHALL:

- preserve Engineering Understanding;
- enforce Architectural Invariants;
- respect Architectural Constraints;
- remain within the defined Non-Goals;
- preserve explainability;
- preserve traceability;
- support human engineering authority.

---

# 4. Mandatory Requirements

An implementation SHALL:

## CF-001

Maintain a single canonical Engineering Understanding for every project state.

---

## CF-002

Support continuous Engineering Cognition.

---

## CF-003

Maintain evidence traceability.

---

## CF-004

Preserve engineering context.

---

## CF-005

Maintain explainable reasoning.

---

## CF-006

Preserve Engineering Memory.

---

## CF-007

Support continuous learning.

---

## CF-008

Maintain historical continuity.

---

## CF-009

Support multidisciplinary engineering cognition.

---

## CF-010

Preserve human engineering authority.

---

# 5. Prohibited Behaviors

A conformant implementation SHALL NOT:

- maintain multiple independent Engineering Understandings;
- discard engineering context;
- perform untraceable reasoning;
- fabricate certainty without evidence;
- bypass human engineering authority;
- violate Architectural Invariants;
- assume responsibilities defined as Non-Goals.

---

# 6. Implementation Independence

Conformance SHALL NOT depend upon:

- programming language;
- operating system;
- database technology;
- deployment architecture;
- cloud provider;
- communication protocol;
- AI model;
- runtime environment.

Different implementations MAY satisfy this specification provided they preserve the architectural requirements defined herein.

---

# 7. Levels of Conformance

The specification defines two conformance levels.

## Core Conformance

Core Conformance requires implementation of all mandatory architectural requirements defined in this specification.

Core implementations preserve the identity of the Hivemind.

---

## Extended Conformance

Extended Conformance includes all Core requirements and additional optional capabilities that enhance Engineering Understanding without violating this specification.

Optional capabilities SHALL remain subordinate to the Architectural Invariants and Constraints.

---

# 8. Conformance Assessment

A conformance assessment SHALL evaluate:

- architectural structure;
- cognitive behavior;
- Engineering Understanding;
- evidence management;
- reasoning transparency;
- traceability;
- governance;
- human collaboration.

Assessment SHALL focus on architectural outcomes rather than implementation details.

---

# 9. Non-Conformance

An implementation is non-conformant if it:

- violates one or more Architectural Invariants;
- ignores mandatory Constraints;
- performs prohibited behaviors;
- abandons Engineering Understanding as the architectural core.

Non-conformance SHALL be explicitly identified and documented.

---

# 10. Future Compatibility

Future revisions of the Hivemind Specification SHALL preserve backward architectural compatibility whenever practical.

Where compatibility cannot be maintained, the specification SHALL identify:

- affected requirements;
- migration guidance;
- revised conformance expectations.

---

# 11. Relationship to Other Specifications

Conformance depends upon every preceding governance specification.

Implementations SHALL be interpreted in accordance with:

- HS-009 – Reference Architecture;
- HS-010 – Architectural Invariants;
- HS-011 – Architectural Constraints;
- HS-012 – Non-Goals.

---

# 12. Summary

Conformance defines the architectural threshold that distinguishes a true Atlas Hivemind implementation from an arbitrary engineering software system.

A conformant implementation is identified not by its technology stack, but by its preservation of Engineering Understanding, evidence-based cognition, architectural governance, and human-centered engineering principles.

---

> **Conformance is measured by architectural fidelity, not implementation similarity. Two implementations may differ completely in technology yet remain equally conformant if they preserve the same architectural principles.**
