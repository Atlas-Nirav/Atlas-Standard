# 1. Purpose

This specification defines the Architectural Invariants of the Atlas Hivemind.

Architectural Invariants are permanent truths that govern every compliant implementation of the Hivemind.

Unlike implementation decisions, these invariants are immutable. They remain valid regardless of programming language, deployment model, infrastructure, artificial intelligence technology, or future architectural evolution.

Any implementation that violates one or more Architectural Invariants is not considered conformant with the Atlas Hivemind Specification.

---

# 2. Definition

An Architectural Invariant is a property that SHALL always remain true throughout the lifecycle of every engineering project.

Architectural Invariants define the constitutional laws of the Engineering Mind.

They are independent of implementation.

They are permanent.

---

# 3. Categories of Invariants

The Hivemind defines six categories of Architectural Invariants:

- Understanding Invariants
- Evidence Invariants
- Context Invariants
- Reasoning Invariants
- Knowledge Invariants
- Governance Invariants

Each category preserves a fundamental property of Engineering Cognition.

---

# 4. Understanding Invariants

## AI-001 — Single Engineering Understanding

Exactly one Engineering Understanding SHALL exist for a single project state.

Multiple independent representations of engineering truth are prohibited.

---

## AI-002 — Canonical Representation

Engineering Understanding SHALL be the canonical cognitive representation of the project.

Every subsystem SHALL reference this understanding rather than maintaining an independent truth.

---

## AI-003 — Continuous Evolution

Engineering Understanding SHALL evolve continuously as engineering reality changes.

Static understanding is non-conformant.

---

## AI-004 — Lifecycle Continuity

Engineering Understanding SHALL persist throughout the complete engineering lifecycle, from conception through operation and historical preservation.

---

# 5. Evidence Invariants

## AI-005 — Evidence Before Conclusion

Every engineering conclusion SHALL be supported by engineering evidence.

Unsupported conclusions are prohibited.

---

## AI-006 — Traceable Evidence

Every item of evidence SHALL remain traceable to its origin.

The origin, timestamp, and provenance of evidence SHALL be preserved.

---

## AI-007 — Preservation of Evidence

Evidence SHALL never be destroyed without preserving historical traceability.

---

# 6. Context Invariants

## AI-008 — Context Preservation

Engineering context SHALL accompany engineering evidence throughout the Cognitive Cycle.

Loss of context is prohibited.

---

## AI-009 — Intent Preservation

Engineering intent SHALL remain associated with Engineering Understanding throughout the project lifecycle.

---

## AI-010 — Relationship Integrity

Relationships between engineering entities SHALL remain explicit and consistent.

Implicit relationships SHALL NOT be treated as canonical.

---

# 7. Reasoning Invariants

## AI-011 — Explainable Reasoning

Every engineering conclusion SHALL be explainable.

The reasoning process SHALL remain available for inspection.

---

## AI-012 — Confidence Representation

Every engineering conclusion SHALL include an explicit confidence assessment.

Confidence SHALL never be implied.

---

## AI-013 — Uncertainty Preservation

Unknown or conflicting information SHALL remain explicitly represented.

The Hivemind SHALL NOT fabricate certainty where sufficient evidence does not exist.

---

## AI-014 — Human Authority

Final engineering decisions SHALL remain the responsibility of qualified human engineers.

The Hivemind augments engineering judgment but does not replace it.

---

# 8. Knowledge Invariants

## AI-015 — Historical Preservation

Historical engineering knowledge SHALL remain permanently accessible.

Knowledge SHALL accumulate over time.

---

## AI-016 — Learning is Additive

Learning SHALL augment Engineering Understanding.

Learning SHALL NOT erase validated historical knowledge.

---

## AI-017 — Organizational Memory

Engineering Memory SHALL preserve organizational knowledge independently of individual projects.

---

# 9. Governance Invariants

## AI-018 — No Independent Truth

No subsystem SHALL maintain an engineering truth independent of the Hivemind.

Engineering Understanding is the single cognitive authority.

---

## AI-019 — Architectural Independence

Architectural Invariants SHALL remain independent of implementation technologies.

No invariant may depend upon a specific software framework, protocol, database, or AI model.

---

## AI-020 — Conformance Preservation

Future architectural evolution SHALL preserve every invariant defined by this specification unless superseded through formal specification revision.

---

# 10. Violation Handling

If an implementation detects a violation of an Architectural Invariant, it SHALL:

- record the violation;
- preserve traceability;
- prevent silent corruption of Engineering Understanding;
- notify the responsible engineering authority;
- maintain historical evidence of the violation.

Architectural Invariants SHALL never fail silently.

---

# 11. Relationship to Other Specifications

This specification governs all Atlas Hivemind specifications.

Every specification SHALL be interpreted in a manner consistent with these Architectural Invariants.

If two specifications appear to conflict, the Architectural Invariants take precedence.

---

# 12. Conformance

An implementation conforms to HS-010 if it:

- preserves all Architectural Invariants;
- continuously enforces them;
- detects invariant violations;
- maintains traceability of violations;
- prevents violations from silently corrupting Engineering Understanding.

---

# 13. Summary

Architectural Invariants define the constitutional laws of the Atlas Hivemind.

They establish the permanent properties that preserve Engineering Understanding, evidence integrity, context, reasoning transparency, organizational knowledge, and human engineering authority.

Unlike implementation details, these invariants do not change with technology.

They define what it fundamentally means to implement the Atlas Hivemind.

---

> **Technology evolves. Implementations change. Architectural Invariants endure. They are the constitutional laws that preserve the integrity of the Engineering Mind.**
