# 1. Purpose

This specification formally defines Engineering Understanding.

Engineering Understanding is the primary cognitive product maintained by the Atlas Hivemind.

Every engineering observation, engineering capability, engineering decision, engineering relationship, engineering memory, engineering rule, and engineering learning process ultimately contributes to Engineering Understanding.

Engineering Understanding is therefore the canonical representation of every engineering project within Atlas.

No other representation shall supersede Engineering Understanding.

---

# 2. Definition

Engineering Understanding is the continuously evolving cognitive representation of an engineering project.

It integrates validated evidence, contextual knowledge, engineering intent, relationships, constraints, reasoning, historical memory, confidence, and explainability into one coherent representation.

Unlike traditional engineering models, Engineering Understanding represents not only what exists, but why it exists, how it behaves, how it relates to other systems, and how that understanding evolves over time.

Engineering Understanding is not a document.

It is not a model.

It is not a database.

It is not a graph.

It is the living cognitive representation of engineering reality.

---

# 3. Purpose of Engineering Understanding

Engineering projects generate enormous quantities of information.

Information alone cannot answer engineering questions.

Engineers require understanding.

Engineering Understanding exists to answer questions such as:

- What exists?
- Why does it exist?
- How does it work?
- What relationships exist?
- What constraints apply?
- What assumptions were made?
- What evidence supports this conclusion?
- How confident are we?
- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- What will happen if it changes again?

Engineering Understanding transforms engineering information into engineering intelligence.

---

# 4. Canonical Representation

Engineering Understanding is the single canonical representation of a project inside Atlas.

Every subsystem contributes toward it.

Every subsystem consumes it.

There shall never exist multiple conflicting Engineering Understandings for the same project state.

Where conflicting evidence exists, the conflict shall become part of Engineering Understanding until resolved.

The Hivemind maintains exactly one coherent Engineering Understanding for every project.

---

# 5. Characteristics

Engineering Understanding possesses the following characteristics.

## 5.1 Continuous

Engineering Understanding evolves continuously.

It never becomes complete.

Every validated observation may improve it.

---

## 5.2 Contextual

Every engineering fact exists within context.

Context includes:

- location
- time
- function
- regulations
- dependencies
- stakeholders
- environment

Understanding cannot exist independently of context.

---

## 5.3 Evidence-Based

Every engineering conclusion shall be supported by traceable evidence.

Evidence may originate from:

- BIM
- CAD
- Sensors
- Human engineers
- Simulations
- Site inspections
- Building codes
- Engineering standards
- Calculations

Evidence remains linked to understanding.

---

## 5.4 Explainable

Every engineering conclusion must remain explainable.

Engineering Understanding preserves:

- reasoning
- evidence
- assumptions
- confidence
- history
- dependencies

Nothing exists without explanation.

---

## 5.5 Evolvable

Understanding continuously changes.

Understanding improves.

Understanding corrects itself.

Understanding never freezes.

---

## 5.6 Human-Centered

Engineering Understanding exists to augment engineers.

Human engineers remain the final authority.

Engineering Understanding shall enable Atlas to answer engineering questions.

"What does it depend upon?"

"What regulations affect it?"

"What happens if it changes?"

"What evidence supports this?"

# 11. Relationship to Engineering Memory

Engineering Memory preserves engineering history.

Engineering Understanding represents the current cognitive state.

Memory provides historical perspective.

Understanding provides present comprehension.

Together they support reasoning.

---

# 12. Relationship to Knowledge

Knowledge consists of engineering facts.

Understanding interprets those facts.

Knowledge answers:

"What is known?"

Understanding answers:

"What does it mean?"

Knowledge supports understanding.

Understanding applies knowledge.

---

# 13. Relationship to Engineering Capabilities

Engineering Capabilities generate specialized engineering evidence.

Engineering Understanding integrates those contributions.

Capabilities think within disciplines.

Engineering Understanding connects disciplines.

---

# 14. Relationship to Decision Support

Engineering Understanding does not make engineering decisions.

Instead it enables better engineering decisions.

Decision quality depends upon understanding quality.

Poor understanding inevitably produces poor engineering decisions.

# 15. Architectural Principles

Engineering Understanding follows these principles.

---

## Continuous Evolution

Understanding never stops improving.

---

## Evidence Before Opinion

---

## Context Before Meaning

# 16. Invariants

Engineering Understanding shall always remain:

- coherent
- explainable
- traceable
- contextual
- evidence-based
- continuously evolving
- internally consistent
- multidisciplinary
- human-centered

These invariants shall never be violated.

---

# 17. Conformance

An implementation conforms to HS-005 if it:

- maintains one Engineering Understanding per project;
- continuously evolves that understanding;
- integrates multidisciplinary evidence;
- preserves engineering context;
- preserves engineering intent;
- preserves engineering relationships;
- supports explainability;
- supports confidence assessment;
- maintains traceability;
- remains internally consistent.

Failure to satisfy these requirements constitutes non-conformance.
