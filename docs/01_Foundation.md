# The Foundation of Atlas

Everything we build must be consistent with these principles.

These are not product requirements.

They are the foundation upon which every decision, every architecture, every model, every feature, and every line of code should stand.

If a future decision conflicts with this document, the decision should be questioned before the foundation is changed.

---

# Core Beliefs

## 1. Buildings Should Be Understood, Not Merely Represented

A building is more than geometry.

It contains relationships, constraints, engineering intent, history, reasoning, and knowledge.

Atlas exists to understand buildings.

---

## 2. Buildings Are Living Engineering Systems

Buildings continuously evolve throughout their lifecycle.

Planning.

Design.

Construction.

Operation.

Maintenance.

Renovation.

Expansion.

Atlas evolves with them.

---

## 3. Engineering Knowledge Must Compound

Every project creates engineering knowledge.

That knowledge should never disappear.

Every completed project should strengthen every future project.

---

## 4. Continuous Engineering Reasoning

Atlas continuously reasons about engineering consequences.

It should not wait for users to ask obvious questions.

Understanding should always be proactive.

---

## 5. One Building. One Intelligence.

Architecture.

Structure.

MEP.

Construction.

Planning.

Operations.

Every engineering discipline contributes to one shared engineering intelligence.

Buildings should be understood as complete systems.

---

## 6. Engineering Before Assumptions

Atlas never invents engineering facts.

Every recommendation must be supported by engineering principles, evidence, or clearly stated assumptions.

Uncertainty should be communicated, never hidden.

---

## 7. Human Expertise Is Amplified, Never Replaced

Atlas exists to empower engineers, architects, designers, contractors, and owners.

Human judgment remains essential.

Atlas strengthens decision-making.

---

## 8. Knowledge Endures

Technology changes.

Software changes.

Artificial Intelligence changes.

Engineering knowledge endures.

Atlas is built upon enduring engineering principles rather than temporary technologies.

---

# Our Promise

Atlas will never optimize for hype over engineering.

Atlas will never prioritize convenience over correctness.

Atlas will never sacrifice explainability for automation.

Atlas will always strive to understand before it recommends.

---

# One Guiding Question

Before building anything, ask:

> **Does this help Atlas better understand the building?**

If the answer is no, reconsider why it exists.
