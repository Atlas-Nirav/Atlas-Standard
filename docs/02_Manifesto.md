# Atlas Manifesto

---

We believe buildings deserve to be understood, not merely represented.

We believe every building is a living engineering system that grows, changes, and accumulates knowledge throughout its lifetime.

We believe engineering knowledge should never disappear when a project ends.

Every drawing, every calculation, every decision, every lesson learned should strengthen the future of the built environment.

We believe software should understand engineering, not simply store information.

We believe intelligence emerges from connecting disciplines rather than separating them.

Architecture.

Structural Engineering.

MEP.

Construction.

Planning.

Project Management.

Sustainability.

Together, they form one engineering intelligence.

We believe technology is temporary.

Engineering principles endure.

Atlas exists to preserve those principles, continuously reason about them, and make them accessible to every engineer, architect, designer, builder, owner, and future generation.

Atlas does not replace engineering judgment.

Atlas amplifies it.

Atlas does not wait to be asked.

Atlas continuously understands.

Atlas does not guess.

Atlas reasons.

Atlas does not chase trends.

Atlas builds enduring engineering intelligence.

Every feature we create must help Atlas better understand the built environment.

Every model we train must preserve engineering knowledge.

Every decision we make must move humanity toward better buildings.

Because every building should make the next building better.

---

## Our Promise

We will build with integrity.

We will engineer with evidence.

We will communicate uncertainty honestly.

We will preserve knowledge relentlessly.

We will never sacrifice engineering correctness for convenience.

We will never stop learning.
